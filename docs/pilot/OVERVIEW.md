# UPWIND — proposed pilot

UPWIND is a local browser application helping community groups explore PM2.5 observations and compare feasible next monitoring sites. It keeps originals, practical site constraints, comparative plans and limits together in a reproducible investigation. A complete synthetic example works without credentials or hardware.

**What a partner can evaluate today:** import an existing CSV campaign; inspect quality and episodes; mark sites with actual permission, power and safe access; compare spatial coverage plans; review a self-contained evidence summary. Experimental source scenarios are optional, conditional and not field validated. The initial nominal identifiable synthetic test did not reliably localize its source.

**Proposed exercise:** retrospective comparison on a campaign the group already holds, with consent and appropriate licensing. Agree on campaign/site/time splits before selection. Expose only readings and weather available at the historical decision time, withhold candidate outcomes until a plan is locked, and compare against what the group actually did plus fair baselines. Follow with an optional planning exercise if users find it useful. The current numerical utility comparison alone does not implement or validate this external retrospective protocol.

**Questions:** Does it change a monitoring decision? Are the costs/access constraints realistic? Are uncertainty and limitations understandable? Would the group use it again? What made the workflow difficult?

**Data and privacy:** files stay on the local machine; no automatic account, upload or external submission. Full JSON includes exact coordinates and raw text; review consent and redact residential details before sharing. The HTML report rounds resident locations but preserves user notes for review.

This is an invitation to evaluate, not an existing partnership. No source attribution, regulatory certification, adoption, health benefit or award outcome is claimed. No outreach has been sent.
