# Supplementary passive concentration controls

Declared while the frozen final run is ongoing, after inspecting source-error
counts but before reviewing its concentration scores. These controls supplement
rather than replace the predeclared old/new comparison or primary targets. No
engine, threshold, final case or source metric may change.

Compare against two formulas with no fitted spatial source: (1) the arithmetic
mean of usable observed concentrations in the last campaign period, using the
same quality review as inference; (2) the fixed10µg/m³ background-prior mean.
Neither sees target outcomes or evaluator weather/source truth. Each predicts
the same constant at all12 unseen target instruments at the last observed time.
They predict concentration only; they do not produce a source location or region.

Evaluate all80scheduled worlds (the first four final seed blocks ×20conditions).
Report their80-world results and separate paired comparisons restricted to each
engine's scored worlds, explicitly retaining its unavailable count. Report world
RMSE and paired seed-block mean differences with5000bootstrap resamples and
seed90606; four blocks are weak evidence about tails. This is post-freeze
supplementary reporting, not a retrospectively selected success criterion.
