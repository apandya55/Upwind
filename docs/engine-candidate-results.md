# Engine 0.7 candidate results

Candidate model **plume-robust-0.7.1**, protocol **UW-ENGINE-2026-07A**. The
protocol document was written before the development split; the source freeze
was recorded before calibration and final evaluation. The
candidate adds humidity-response covariance, shared-weather bounded two-source
adequacy, temporal/spatial/heavy-tail residual checks, and explicit refusal when
an adequacy diagnostic is unavailable. It does not alter `evaluations/engine-v06`.

## Splits and denominators

| Split | Seed range | Worlds | Families | Attempts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | 8,110,000–8,110,015 | 16 | 8 | 128 |
| Calibration | 8,210,000–8,210,015 | 16 | 8 | 128 |
| Final | 8,310,000–8,310,031 | 32 | 8 | 256 |

Families are nominal, humidity response, sensor drift, changing release, two
sources, spatial background, heavy tail and weather error. A refusal or crash
stays in the attempt denominator. The pre-repair final run had 255 successful
serializations and one visible non-finite NNLS crash. The post-final repair reran
the same locked worlds and produced 256/256 finite rows; it is an engineering
confirmation, not new accuracy evidence.

The three conditional spatial-background support rows contained the simulated
source in their 90% finite region, yet their posterior-mean errors were about
172–197 m. No two-source pair was materially accepted. Two sensor-drift rows had
non-finite competing-source fits; the repaired diagnostic recorded the issue
and withheld source support. These outcomes are synthetic and do not estimate
field accuracy.

## Matched development comparison

The compact paired comparison in
`evaluations/engine-v07/matched-development` gives both engines the same 16
fresh worlds (two seeds × eight families), generated by the v0.7 harness. Truth
is used only after both fits to score the report. The archived v0.6 source is
loaded from `evaluations/engine-v06/baseline-source`; its frozen results are not
rewritten.

| Metric | v0.6.0 | v0.7.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Successful fits | 16/16 | 16/16 |
| `source_supported` | 0/16 | 0/16 |
| 90% region contains truth | 16/16 | 16/16 |
| Median posterior-mean error | 97.5 m | 90.8 m |
| P90 posterior-mean error | 172.3 m | 167.9 m |
| Median density-mode error | 0.0 m* | 458.3 m |
| Median 90% region area | 89.1% | 89.1% |

`*` The archived v0.6 public response did not expose its posterior mean/mode;
the comparator scores its location-weighted source cells, which makes the mode
comparison a representation diagnostic rather than a like-for-like point
summary. The small 16-world sample was not used to tune thresholds and does
not demonstrate a general improvement. It does show that v0.7 preserved broad
conditional coverage on these worlds while adding refusal diagnostics; it did
not meet the 50 m point-error target.

The v0.7 stress harness records observable inference status and keeps evaluator
truth separate; it does not score held-out concentration predictions or planner
losses. Production diagnostics expose withheld-sensor/window checks, predictive
tail resolution and unavailable blocks, but those checks are conditional
adequacy screens rather than an independent prediction benchmark. Existing v0.6
passive-prediction and planning results therefore remain separate historical
evidence and are not presented as matched v0.7 performance.

## What is and is not established

The candidate fixes a real numerical failure and makes several externality
signals visible. The final evidence does not show a precision breakthrough:
all nominal worlds remained ambiguous, accepted support was rare, and accepted
spatial-background means were far from the simulated source. Prediction metrics
and source-location metrics answer different questions; neither supports source
responsibility. The planner remains a conditional observed-window score and
has no future-weather or field-benefit claim.

Measured validation is still blocked. Official NOAA/DATEM/Sagebrush routes were
unavailable through the environment proxy, and the inspected Prairie Grass
workbook lacks the provenance, units and compatible meteorology needed for a
source-localization benchmark. No measured observations were fitted.

## Reproduction

Run the focused backend checks and the paired comparator with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=backend OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 \
  .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q backend/tests/test_engine_v07.py \
  backend/tests/test_post_release_integrity.py

PYTHONPATH=backend:. OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 \
  .venv/bin/python scripts/compare_v06_v07.py \
  --out .local/engine-v07-matched-development-repro
```

The comparator refuses an existing output directory. The locked v0.7 harness
can be reproduced only into a new append-only directory, for example:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=backend OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 \
  .venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_v07.py \
  --split development --limit 1 --out .local/engine-v07-new-smoke
```

The existing final artifacts and their hashes are recorded in
`evaluations/engine-v07/source-freeze.json` and
`evaluations/engine-v07/post-final-integrity.json`.
