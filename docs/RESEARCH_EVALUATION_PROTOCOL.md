## Questions and hypotheses

1. Nuisance-profile/dense-oracle diagnostics will separate missing information from numerical or prior defects. Correct physics/wind/background is diagnostic truth access, never deployable inference.
2. Fixed monitors sampled under explicitly separate, individually steady wind windows can distinguish a constant release better than repeating one wind. This is an **instrumentation/data-collection intervention**, not evidence that a new estimator recovers missing information from the old data. Domain, release strengths and monitor/candidate budgets do not change around truth.
3. A deterministic Bayes-linear spatial-prediction risk objective can improve realized held-out concentration prediction without the old acquisition's component reduction or noisy finite-mixture entropy rankings. It is a separate objective from localization; report both tasks.
4. User-declared meteorological accuracy and physical heights should enter the equations explicitly. They are documented inputs, not quantities extracted from evaluator truth or inferred instrument calibration.
