# Engine overhaul evaluation protocol

Protocol **UW-OVERHAUL-2026-01**, declared 2026-10-18 before running the redesigned model on these cases. Scientific target: `plume-mixture-0.3.0`. This protocol cannot establish field calibration or attribution. All previously inspected cases, including the 220000-series stress cases, are now development data.

## Frozen separation and supported population

Development seeds are **410000–410011**, calibration seeds **510000–510023**, and final seeds **610000–610039**. A seed identifies a simulated world shared across methods, not an independent dataset for each method. The generator is evaluator-only, with truth kept out of every `Investigation`; production must not import it. No threshold or prior will be selected from final outcomes. After a final result informs a production change, that result becomes development-used and requires a newly declared evaluation or an explicit statement that untouched evaluation no longer remains.

Each split contains ten conditions: `background_only`, `weak_signal`, `strong_signal`, `direction_bias`, `two_sources`, `boundary`, `correlated_errors`, `episodic`, `missingness`, and `unsupported_weather`. The primary final experiment has **400 worlds** (40 per condition). Forty independent worlds give a worst-case 95% binomial half-width of about 15 percentage points; this resolves large failures, not small superiority or broad environmental generalization. Wilson intervals accompany event rates. Report distributions and paired differences, without treating repeated methods or readings as independent worlds.

Development uses a symmetric seven-monitor layout, Gaussian lateral profile, latitude 52°, wind from 260–280°. Calibration uses a six-monitor staggered layout, stretched-exponential exponent 2.2, latitude 36°, wind from 255–260° or 280–285°. Final uses an asymmetric six-monitor layout, exponent 1.65, latitude 41°, wind from 245–255° or 285–295°, different lateral/vertical growth coefficients, and nonuniform errors. Thus complete layout/transport/weather regimes are held out, rather than merely RNG seeds. This is a controlled transport mismatch challenge, not a sample of all real sites.

The local source rectangle is x∈[-450,-80] m and y∈[-220,220] m, specified by nine anchors. Continuous source coordinates are drawn between anchors (boundary cases within 5 m of a boundary). Every generated source is off the old discrete grid almost surely. Sensors/candidates lie east of the rectangle, all within 3 km. Primary source strength is 18,000–36,000 µg/s, weak strength 150–450 µg/s, source height 7 m, receptor height 2 m. Background is 9–16 µg/m³ with a small drift. Every origin is explicitly synthetic. The final weather record is historical contemporaneous weather available before placement; there is no forecast or future scheduling claim.

## Independent transport and observations

Evaluation does not call the production plume kernel. It uses a flux-normalized crosswind generalized-normal density `p exp(-|y/sy|^p)/(2 sy Γ(1/p))`, a reflected vertical Gaussian density, and division by wind speed. Dispersion widths include a finite near-field intercept and different power-law growth from production. Source height, lateral shape, and growth differ across splits. Concentrations are averaged over five fixed sub-window wind perturbations (−4,−2,0,2,4°), rather than evaluating a single steady centerline. Wind-from is independently transformed with sine/cosine; eastward flow at 270° is checked in evaluator self-checks. A second implementation with the same Gaussian assumptions would not count as this transport challenge.

Instrument errors include persistent per-sensor bias, an explicitly simulated space/time correlated field, and independent heteroscedastic reading noise. Correlated-error cases increase correlation lengths and field amplitude; episodic cases switch the release off during alternating epochs; two-source cases add a second continuous source with 0.8–1.2 times the first strength. Background-only has exactly zero release. Missingness removes a reproducible 35% of readings and every reading from one monitor. Unsupported cases alternate calm wind and >25° within-window direction variability, and remain in all retention denominators.

The generator saves hidden source(s), transport settings and realized errors only in evaluator artifacts. Production inputs have neutral names and IDs, with no family, seed, source truth, withheld targets or future outcomes in names, notes, settings seed or metadata. The planner's ordinary seed is fixed at 17 in every world. Generator seed and family are external evaluator fields.

## Metrics fixed before outcomes

For source-containing worlds, report median, 10th/90th percentiles and full empirical localization errors using the new conditional source-cell mean/MAP center and the old discrete MAP (ties reported). A region is the union of complete rectangular cells in descending conditional single-source posterior mass until at least 0.9. Its coverage concerns the primary continuous source conditional on the single-source explanation; it is not a geographic responsibility probability. Report actual accumulated conditional mass, area in m², area/domain ratio, coverage, and width/usefulness together. Two-source coverage of the primary source is descriptive under a false model, not calibrated coverage. Old grid scenario sets are not geographic regions and receive no interpolated area/coverage claim.

Report background-only source false alarms at source-model weight >0.9, source misses at weight <0.5, source-model Brier score, conditional-region misses with area <25% of domain, and wrong sharp regions (source-model weight >0.9 plus such a region missing truth). Thresholds are reporting definitions, not tuned acceptance rules. A nominal weight or 90% set remains conditional on assumptions; no empirical calibration is asserted from its label. Include source-probability reliability bins [0,.2,.4,.6,.8,1] with counts.

Hold out an entire sensor for prediction (never randomly adjacent readings), cycling the sorted sensor ID by world index so that the target is not always a distant low-signal receptor. Report mixture predictive marginal 90% interval coverage, mean negative log predictive density, RMSE and interval width against its observed readings. New intervals use component predictive covariance, not only between-component spread. Old intervals use its documented observation variance and nuisance mixture; missing capability is explicit. Report model warning rate per family, false alarms on no-source/strong single-source conditions, and misses on two-source/episodic/direction-bias conditions, while acknowledging observational indistinguishability. Status/refusal/crash counts and retained fractions use all generated cases.

Perturbation stability, on the first four seeds of each supported condition, compares original inputs with +3° measured wind, +0.5 µg/m³ readings, and reversed row/site/anchor order. Record model weight change, source estimate displacement, region symmetric-difference fraction and plan overlap (plans on the first seed only, inference sensitivity on all four). These perturbations test sensitivity and invariance, not truth recovery.

## Placement experiment

Use the first **12 final seeds** in `strong_signal`, `weak_signal`, and `direction_bias` (36 paired worlds). Lock all choices before generating any candidate outcomes. Compare redesigned planner, preserved 0.2.1 planner, maximin spread, applicable wind baseline and **20 random seeds (7000–7019)**. Each uses identical permissible candidate IDs/costs, budget 250, equipment limit 2, available contemporaneous weather and the same sampling window. Candidates have permission, power and access; no recommendation implies travel feasibility beyond those explicit constraints. No future window or routing is claimed.

Candidate outcomes and three independent target readings are then generated jointly under the hidden evaluator transport/error process, conditioned through the pre-generated shared field. Every method receives the same realized outcome for a selected site. New readings are appended at the final observed timestamp, with their explicit uncertainty and synthetic origin; each method is refitted with the same redesigned engine. This isolates acquisition quality. Preserve old-planner source estimates separately; do not conflate an acquisition comparison with comparison of entire inference pipelines.

Report paired localization-error and held-out-target RMSE changes, predictive log scores, region area/coverage, source-weight changes, cost and number purchased. Compare each method to the per-world mean of the 20 random selections, and report how often redesigned placement is worse, with Wilson intervals. Entropy reduction alone is not benefit. If a method refuses or selects nothing, retain that outcome and score the unchanged investigation when fitting is possible. Tiny-plan optimizer correctness and redundant-measurement checks belong to engineering tests separately.

## Subsampling, ablations, resources and provenance

Inspect all loaded rows before selection. Report loaded/quality-excluded/fitted counts separately for every case. Compare regular balanced selection with full-data fits where the public inference API exposes an explicit row limit, for 24, 48, 96 and 192-row generated investigations; preserve a rare high-release epoch. If full-data fitting is unsupported, report that unresolved limitation rather than relabeling a 40-row fit as full-data. Ablations must use development/calibration data only, with documented model/configuration switches. A disabled feature must alter calculations, not only a display label.

Before final execution save SHA256 of production Python sources, evaluator, protocol, requirements, exact command and split configuration. Keep output directories append-only. Save every per-world result, including failures, and raw paired planner outcomes. Record Python/dependency versions, CPU, platform and peak RSS. An existing output directory is never silently overwritten. Baseline source is the preserved `evaluations/overhaul/baseline-source` snapshot and its manifest. Exact evaluator command:

```
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python -m upwind.validation.overhaul --split final --out evaluations/overhaul/new-final
```

`--split development` and `--split calibration` use their declared disjoint seeds. `--limit-seeds` is allowed only for development/debug runs, which must be labeled partial and must not be called the final experiment. `--skip-planner` is a recorded explicit reduction in scope. `--supplementary-only --split development` runs background-only/no-wind-uncertainty ablations and the declared sample/full comparisons on development seed 410000/410001; these settings do not tune final thresholds. Final run begins only after the production model and protocol are frozen; corrections to an evaluator bug preserve original outputs and document the independent justification.

Measured-data status is separate. Inspect official NOAA DATEM/Project Sagebrush routes, units, release/source coordinates and license; preserve exact acquisition failures. The existing London single-station audit cannot validate source localization. Successful tracer evaluation would support only the compatible tracer task, not neighborhood PM2.5 attribution.
