# UPWIND engine implementation plan

## 1. Executive decision

For the next candidate, retain a finite Bayesian comparison of background-only and one steady source, with the full conditional distribution over source cells, shared weather alternatives, and positive release strength. Keep the existing analytic Gaussian integration of linear background/trend and random-effect terms. Make the geographic and weather integration demonstrably convergent, report multimodality and numerical uncertainty, and withhold resolved support when the integration does not converge. This is the strongest inference the current observations and declared model can justify without inventing calibration data or a source-count prior.

The output estimates a source-location distribution **conditional on** the declared domain, neutral steady plume, weather prior, covariance model, and listed hypotheses. It cannot establish that those hypotheses include the real process, that a source caused an observation, or that a location is accurate in the field. Preserve the one-source distribution and adequacy flags as separate evidence. Do not turn the bounded pair diagnostic into a probability of two sources without a predeclared prior and evaluation supporting that model comparison. Do not tune thresholds to increase acceptance or meet a distance target.

The immediate scientific change is deterministic quadrature convergence and an explicit numerical-convergence gate, not a new transport law or high-dimensional sampler. If the posterior remains broad or multimodal after numerical convergence, report that result; more optimization cannot recover information absent from the observations.

## 2. Current engine trace

1. `POST /api/import/csv` in `backend/upwind/api.py` calls `importers.parse_csv()`; `importers.utc_timestamp()` requires a timezone for local timestamps and converts aware values to UTC. `Measurement` and `Weather` in `models.py` retain row origin, humidity, uncertainties, flags, station lineage, and provenance. `POST /api/analyze` validates an `Investigation` and calls `engine.analyze()`.
2. `quality.measurement_quality()` excludes negative, flagged, and >500 µg/m³ review rows, quarantines conflicting sensor/time duplicates, and deduplicates identical rows. `quality.select_rows()` retains every usable row up to the inference limit; above it, deterministic sensor or sensor×window sampling retains prioritized peaks/endpoints and seeded time strata. Raw rows remain stored. `campaign.assignment()` supplies explicit steady windows; UTC is used throughout.
3. `weather.matched_weather()` associates each retained measurement with primary weather within 30 minutes and 3 km and checks the full local window for wind speed 0.8–8 m/s, direction deviation ≤25°, and speed ratio ≤2. `campaign.observed_weather()` and `weather_evidence.joint_weather_grid()` preserve coherent direction/speed alternatives and, where qualified paired comparisons exist, their declared common-mode uncertainty. Wind direction is meteorological “from,” clockwise from north; `numerics.flow_vector()` converts it to downwind east/north flow. Weather remains user-supplied and representativeness is unverified absent independent evidence.
4. `inference.fit()` projects WGS84 to local azimuthal-equidistant metres (x east, y north), checks 20 m–3 km source/receptor support, and constructs source cells over the rectangle spanned by anchors. Prior mass is proportional to cell area. The forward kernel in `numerics.plume()` uses `Q` in µg/s, speed in m/s, and returns µg/m³. It is a ground-reflected neutral Gaussian plume with default release/receptor heights 5 m/2 m, `σy=.08x/sqrt(1+.0001x)` and `σz=.06x/sqrt(1+.0015x)`, with zero plume outside its downwind/radial support. The coefficients are project assumptions; the inspected research record does not validate them for field or urban PM2.5.
5. For observations `y`, `fit()` builds an explicit covariance for supplied measurement uncertainty, persistent sensor offset, per-window background and trend, exponentially correlated space/time model error, and the v0.7 humidity-response random coefficient. `gaussian_design.grouped_design()` whitens repeated transport signatures once. The source strength is HalfNormal(scale 30,000 µg/s), marginalized analytically; finite source/weather alternatives are combined with log-sum-exp. Speed scaling uses the exact `Q/speed_scale` change of variable and rescales the strength prior. The prior probability of background-only versus source is 0.5/0.5.
6. Source location is integrated using midpoint or four-node cell quadrature; current refinement in `source_grid.subdivide()` splits one posterior-directed set of cells once and retains all other area mass. `location_summary()` returns posterior mean, density mode, covariance/RMS, edge mass, and a 90% conditional single-source region. The mean may lie between modes; the region is a finite-model mass set, not a calibrated geographic confidence region.
7. `predictive_checks.screen()` refits after withholding selected whole sensors/windows and runs a finite Sobol joint predictive-tail screen. `identifiability.diagnose_fit()` computes a GLS profile and bounded 24-cell, shared-weather two-source profile. `_externality_checks()` flags selected residual patterns. These are conditional diagnostics, not proof of model adequacy or identification of an instrument, source, or cause.
8. `engine.analyze()` returns `status="ok"` for a completed fit and separately assigns `evidence_status`: `model_mismatch` if the predictive or paired-weather screen warns; otherwise `source_supported` only if source probability ≥0.9, conditional region area < half the supported domain, the region is not domain-limited, and predictive/weather, externality, and pair diagnostics pass; otherwise `ambiguous`. `report.evidence_report()` and API/export paths serialize the conditional result and warnings. `models.migrate_analysis()` archives results when model or protocol identifiers change.

Finite cell/weather quadrature, one refinement pass, a bounded pair shortlist, sampled predictive blocks, row selection, and threshold diagnostics are approximations. Sensor drift, spatial background, changing release, heavy tails, two sources, and incorrect/shared wind can violate the single steady plume model. Weak signal, correlated sensors, inadequate wind diversity, source-domain truncation, and physically equivalent source/weather explanations are information limits. Better quadrature fixes only the second category.

## 4. Mathematical specification

For retained row `i`, let `y_i` be PM2.5 in µg/m³, `s_i` its supplied standard uncertainty (or configured fallback), `r_i` its projected receptor, `t_i` UTC seconds centered on the campaign, `u_i` UTC seconds centered within its explicit steady window (or `t_i` for a single window), `g_i` sensor ID, and `w_i` steady-window label. Let `x` be source location, `q≥0` release, and `m` a coherent weather-offset/speed scenario. The declared forward mean is `μ_i(x,q,m)=10+b_{w_i}+a_{w_i}u_i/3600+q f_i(x,m)`, where `f_i` is the unit-strength `numerics.plume()` column. The random intercept `b_w~N(0,20²)` µg/m³ and slope `a_w~N(0,10²)` µg/m³/hour are independent by window. Do not estimate or relabel these as field-calibrated values.

Use the current marginalized Gaussian covariance, stated explicitly as

`Σ_ij = δ_ij s_i² + σ² exp(-||r_i-r_j||/150 - |t_i-t_j|/600) + 1² 1[g_i=g_j] + 20² 1[w_i=w_j] + 10² (u_i/3600)(u_j/3600)1[w_i=w_j] + 2² h_i h_j 1[g_i=g_j]`,

with `σ` the configured measurement/model-noise scale and `h_i=max(0,(humidity_i-70)/20)`; missing humidity gives `h_i=0`, not an imputed observation. Keep each covariance component separately inspectable in tests and diagnostics. This is Gaussian random-effect marginalization, so determinant/normalization terms must remain in every marginal likelihood.

Compare `M0: y~N(μ0,Σ)` against `M1(x,m,q): y~N(μ0+q f(x,m),Σ)`, with the declared 0.5 prior on each model, area-uniform source prior on the supported rectangular domain, joint weather prior from `joint_weather_grid()`, and `q~HalfNormal(τ)` where `τ=30,000/speed_scale`. Integrate `q` using the existing rank-one Gaussian update and positive-normal truncation normalizer; integrate nuisance coefficients through `Σ`; integrate `x,m` with area-weighted source/weather quadrature. Preserve all posterior mass and normalize using `logsumexp`. The reported location posterior is `p(x|y,M1, declared assumptions)`, not `p(x|y)` unconditional on source existence.

Retain the full cell/node mass and connected/multimodal structure. Report posterior mean and density mode separately, but do not use either to collapse a broad or separated posterior into a unique answer. Keep the 90% mass region, its area/domain fraction, between-mode structure, posterior covariance, and source-existence weight together. Keep the v0.7 pair and residual diagnostics as named adequacy evidence; they do not become posterior model probabilities or confirmatory tests.

Implement nested deterministic convergence over source-cell and weather-grid refinements. At each level compare log marginal evidence, source-existence weight, cell-mass total-variation distance after mapping to common parent cells, posterior mean/covariance, 90% region mass/area, and weather marginal mass. Predeclare numerical tolerances before inspecting fresh outcomes; tie tolerances to reported map resolution and floating-point/quadrature error, not to source-error targets. Record level, workload, deltas, and convergence. If the maximum supported level is reached without convergence, retain the finite conditional estimate for inspection but mark numerical convergence false and block `source_supported`. A nonconverged integral is not a physical model mismatch.

Unverifiable from current observations alone: neutral rural dispersion coefficients and fixed heights; domain completeness and source count; constant source and open terrain; local wind representativeness and shared-error distribution; covariance scales and humidity response; exchangeability/independence assumptions in priors; row-selection effects; true false-positive/coverage calibration of the support thresholds. No inferred pollution-conditioned wind offset is independent weather calibration.

## 5. Implementation design for Sol

1. **Freeze and version first.** Preserve the dirty v0.7 tree and all existing evidence. Snapshot its scientific source/configuration hashes into a new append-only candidate record. Assign `plume-robust-0.8.0` / `UW-ENGINE-2026-08` (or the next actually unused identifier) before changing inference. Do not overwrite v0.6/v0.7 artifacts or rerun their spent seeds as held-out evidence.
2. **Add convergence machinery.** Add `backend/upwind/quadrature.py` with an internal immutable `QuadraturePolicy` and `QuadratureDiagnostics`; expose `integrate_source_weather(inv, fit_level, policy)` only if this cleanly separates it from `inference.fit()`. Policy fields: initial/max source levels, weather step sequence, max cells/components/design entries, and predeclared numerical tolerances. Diagnostics: level sequence, cell and weather counts, log-evidence delta, source-probability delta, mapped cell-mass TV, mean/covariance delta, region mass/area delta, convergence boolean, and explicit workload/nonconvergence reason. Keep deterministic ordering and area weights at every level.
3. **Refactor `inference.fit()`.** Replace the one-pass `_adaptive` recursion with the nested integration controller while preserving the analytic `q`/Gaussian algebra and exact speed rescaling. Reuse `plume_columns()` and `grouped_design()` at each level; free large design arrays between levels as the current recursion does. Compare posterior masses on common parent-cell IDs, not by matching unstable array indices. Do not drop low-mass cells or weather alternatives to make the diagnostic pass. Return the finest finite result plus `quadrature_diagnostics`.
4. **Gate semantics in `engine.py`.** Keep `status="ok"` for a completed but nonconverged posterior. Add `numerical_converged` and `numerical_refusal_reason` to result metadata; force `evidence_status="ambiguous"` and a specific warning if false. Preserve `model_mismatch` exclusively for current predictive/weather warning rules. Keep current support gates and do not loosen them. Ensure unavailable convergence diagnostics fail closed.
5. **Preserve public and stored contracts.** Keep current fields and add diagnostics additively in `engine.py`; `models.migrate_analysis()` already archives changed model/protocol outputs. Update `report.evidence_report()` and backend serialization tests so report, JSON API, store, and CSV/raw imports continue to distinguish measured values, model predictions, and inferred explanations. Do not alter `importers.py`, UTC normalization, row origins, or provenance. Keep frontend/GIS out of scope; retain current public interfaces for future consumers.
6. **Keep the adequacy diagnostic bounded.** Do not increase the pair shortlist or label its Δχ² cutoff as calibrated. Record when it is unavailable. Any later full two-source posterior needs a separately justified source-count prior, permutation-invariant pair representation, full prior integration, workload analysis, and a new protocol/evaluation.
7. **Record provenance.** Evaluation metadata must include model/protocol, config, source hash, quadrature policy, seed split, eligible/attempted/refused/crashed counts, and output hash. Store truth separately from model-visible investigations. Preserve exact raw rows, timestamps, weather and origin metadata; never let evaluator truth enter `analyze()`.

## 6. Algorithm alternatives considered

| Alternative | Assessment for this repository |
|---|---|
| Current finite Bayesian `M0/M1` mixture with analytic Gaussian/strength integration | Selected. It preserves source-existence uncertainty, correlated observations, shared weather alternatives, and multimodal location mass with dependencies already present. Its finite support makes numerical convergence the immediate gap. All claims remain conditional. |
| MAP/least-squares optimizer | Reject as primary inference. It discards posterior mass, source-existence uncertainty, multimodality, and uncertainty in shared weather; the posterior mean/mode divergence in the development results shows why one point is misleading. Retain profile optimization only as a labeled sensitivity diagnostic. |
| MCMC/NUTS over source, strength, weather, and discrepancy | Not justified now. Discrete/multimodal location plus weakly identified weather/background parameters invite mixing and prior dependence; a new sampler adds convergence and calibration burden without supplying missing information. Simulation-based computation checks do not validate transport physics. |
| Fit a flexible spatial/temporal discrepancy GP or free sensor offsets | Defer. A discrepancy process can absorb the plume and trade off with source location/strength; current observations do not identify its covariance independently. Kennedy & O’Hagan describe Bayesian computer-model calibration with discrepancy, but that is not evidence that this campaign can identify both. Keep fixed, declared covariance and expose sensitivity until external validation exists. |
| Full Bayesian source-count model including two sources | Defer. The current best-24 pair profile is an adequacy warning, not a full integral. A source-count posterior needs an explicit count prior, exchangeability handling, pair integration, and independent evaluation; current sparse data and workload do not justify it. |
| Increase source acceptance or tune error thresholds | Reject. It does not improve inference and would select on already inspected synthetic outcomes. |

References: Gabry, Simpson, Vehtari, Betancourt & Gelman (2019), “Visualization in Bayesian workflow,” *JRSS A* 182:389–402, [doi:10.1111/rssa.12378](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssa.12378), supports separating computation, predictive checks, model criticism, and calibration; it does not validate UPWIND’s plume. Kennedy & O’Hagan (2001), “Bayesian calibration of computer models,” *JRSS B* 63(3):425–464, [doi:10.1111/1467-9868.00294](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9868.00294), motivates explicit caution about confounding model discrepancy with calibration parameters. The inspected official Stan User’s Guide chapters on [finite mixtures](https://github.com/stan-dev/docs/blob/235171991f7040b0b0ebb59e974d1299af5a049b/src/stan-users-guide/finite-mixtures.qmd), [posterior predictive checks](https://github.com/stan-dev/docs/blob/235171991f7040b0b0ebb59e974d1299af5a049b/src/stan-users-guide/posterior-predictive-checks.qmd), and [simulation-based calibration](https://github.com/stan-dev/docs/blob/235171991f7040b0b0ebb59e974d1299af5a049b/src/stan-users-guide/simulation-based-calibration.qmd) inform verification, not field validity. Official [SciPy Sobol documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol.html) applies to existing predictive QMC draws; do not mistake finite QMC tail resolution for calibration. The project’s current plume coefficients have no inspected external validation reference; retain that limitation explicitly.

## 8. Risks and stop conditions

- If source/weather refinement changes evidence weight, region membership, or the dominant mode beyond predeclared tolerances, the current posterior was numerically unresolved. Report nonconvergence and withhold support; do not select whichever grid gives the desired answer.
- If results depend strongly on declared source-strength, weather, background, or covariance priors, report the sensitivity envelope and do not select a prior from the locked final split.
- If posterior remains broad, multimodal, domain-edge limited, or compatible with background-only, the correct result is unresolved conditional location. New independent wind, sensor calibration, layouts, or controlled releases may be needed.
- A flexible discrepancy term, free sensor intercepts, or source-count model can make location less identifiable. Stop if it produces posterior conclusions driven mainly by prior choices or indistinguishable likelihoods; do not describe a numerical posterior as empirical confidence.
- Resource ceilings may prevent fine integration for campaign workloads. Keep exact workload refusals, input rows, and raw data visible. Do not silently reduce cells, weather alternatives, rows, or candidate sources to return a result.
- No compatible measured validation data are currently available. The neutral Gaussian approximation, humidity prior, weather representativeness, and status thresholds remain unvalidated for field PM2.5. This blocks field accuracy, source responsibility, regulatory attribution, or deployment claims regardless of synthetic results.
