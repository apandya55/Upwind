# Three-minute demonstration

**0:00–0:30 — The question.** Open the bundled investigation. Explain that all coordinates, weather and readings are synthetic, and that the purpose is deciding where to measure next, not proving a facility caused pollution. Show the observed-point map and UTC timeline.

**0:30–1:00 — Practical constraints.** Select Footbridge, show permission/power/access and cost. Uncheck permission. Show how it becomes unavailable. Inspect CSV import/Data quality and explain retained originals and flags.

**1:00–1:45 — Compare a real computed plan.** Generate plans. Compare optimized, spread, seeded random and wind methods under the same budget. Reduce budget and rerun to show the selected sites change. Point out that the score is conditional planning utility in nats, not a health benefit or real-world probability.

**1:45–2:15 — Scientific honesty.** Open Scenarios. Show broad weights and the nominal identifiable evaluation failure. Uncheck representative weather, run to demonstrate refusal, then switch to spatial coverage to demonstrate a capability that still works. Future scheduling remains unavailable.

**2:15–2:45 — Reproducible evidence.** Save/reopen. Import a follow-up CSV into a disposable study, inspect its summary, regenerate. Export HTML report and private JSON; show provenance, assumptions, unresolved questions, model/input hash and synthetic labels. Review privacy; do not submit externally.

**2:45–3:00 — What remains.** Independent external benchmark, prospective planner benefit, a better signal/noise study, and actual partner usability testing remain unperformed. No adoption or competition outcome is claimed.
