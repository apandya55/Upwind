How the evidence is built

EPA annual summaries are filtered for completeness and compatible units. Event-treatment alternatives are selected once per instrument; colocated instruments are combined into a physical site-year. Conflicting duplicates fail the export. Missing weather remains null.

The deployed national method uses the nearest eligible monitor. Regional NC benchmarks exclude entire one-degree spatial folds, hold station assignment fixed across years, and compare nearest-monitor, IDW, kernel ridge and training-mean predictions. County, year and distance breakdowns are available. Bootstrap intervals resample stations with all their years; they do not eliminate dependence between neighboring stations.

Limits

Annual averages cannot recover hourly AQI or commute-window peaks. Monitor placement is not population-representative. 2025 observations are provisional. Accuracy at held-out stations does not establish accuracy at every unmonitored location. The county report performs no road-buffer or school-location join.
